# Company Skill: `lifestory-history-scene`

Category: `general_files` · Domain: LifeStory Era Scroll & Everyday Memory Scene

## Purpose

Generate historically grounded urban/rural everyday-life scene specifications and empathetic interview prompts for the LifeStory (`人生几何`) era scroll.

## Mandatory Deliverable Contract

1. **Era & Region Anchor**: Include explicit year/decade (e.g., `1978`, `1992`) and city/province context.
2. **Layered Everyday Scene**: Describe foreground, midground, and background details rooted in ordinary daily life (housing, street furniture, transport, work, clothing, soundscape) rather than generic textbook headlines.
3. **Time-Reporter Prompts (`时光记者`)**: Provide at least 3 open-ended, respectful memory-recall questions that invite an elder to share personal lived experience from that scene.
4. **Historical Honesty**: Mark uncertain regional details explicitly; never fabricate fake archival citations.
